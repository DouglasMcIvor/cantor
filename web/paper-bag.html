ℵ Cantor — Programming your way out of a paper bag

Frances Buontempo used diffusion to escape a paper bag. Add an imaginary unit and the diffusion equation becomes the Schrödinger equation — so this particle escapes a sealed bag by tunneling straight through the wall. Colour is the wave function's phase, brightness its magnitude. Hit Measure to collapse the wave function; sooner or later it collapses outside the bag and you are free. The strip under the grid shows your live chance of escaping if you measure right now — and if the bag is holding you too well, weaken it.
