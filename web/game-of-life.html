ℵ Cantor — Game of Life

Conway's Game of Life, compiled from Cantor to WebAssembly, on a 30×30 torus — a glider walking off one edge reappears on the opposite edge. Every frame is one Event through a proof-carrying event loop whose Output is a bitmap (docs/design-decisions.md §6's Nat * Nat * Unsigned32* convention) rather than text — see the text-based demo for the original shape.
